## The Deep Void 1.99.3.1 for NeoForge 1.21.1

First release of the unofficial port of **[The Deep Void](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/the-deep-void)**
v1.99.3.1 by **Sombrius** (Forge 1.20.1) to **NeoForge 1.21.1**. All the content of 1.99.3.1 is here: the dimension,
17 biomes, 193 entities, the bosses, 991 items, 472 blocks and 58 effects.

### Requirements
- Minecraft **1.21.1**, NeoForge **21.1.197** or newer
- **[GeckoLib](https://modrinth.com/mod/geckolib)** 4.9 or newer (NeoForge 1.21.1)
- Optional: JEI
- Install on both the client and the server

### Notes
- Content and numbers are the same as in 1.20.1; where Minecraft 1.21 works differently the 1.20.1 behaviour is kept
  (enchantments, paintings and music discs are now 1.21 data, undead / arthropod mobs use the 1.21 entity tags,
  projectile knockback and gun arm poses are preserved).
- Fixed for 1.21: Liquid Void / Ichor lakes no longer crash world generation; giant skull structures are clipped at the
  chunk border instead of filling the log with errors.
- Every difference from 1.20.1 and the reason for it: [PORTLOG.md](https://github.com/Vladeezyy/the-deep-void-1.21/blob/main/PORTLOG.md)

### Languages
English, Russian, Simplified Chinese, Spanish and Portuguese (Brazil). The Russian and Chinese translations were
completed and Spanish and Portuguese were added; tooltips that the original wrote in English straight in the code are
translatable now as well.
